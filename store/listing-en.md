## Full description (max 4000)

```
A glowing ball bounces down an endless spiral tower, set against an aurora-lit sky.
There is exactly one control: drag to spin the tower, line the ball up with a gap,
and let it fall.

■ One thumb, three seconds to learn
No tutorial walls, no menus to wade through. One drag and you already know the rules.
Runs are short by design, so it fits a commute or a two-minute break.

■ The deeper you go, the tighter it gets
Matching platforms bounce you back, gaps let you through, red platforms cost a life.
As you descend the gaps narrow and the danger tiles multiply. You get three lives.

■ Five combo, then RAGE
Pass through rings without bouncing and your combo climbs.
Hit five and the ball ignites: for seven straight rings nothing can stop it,
smashing through every platform in its path while the screen shakes and debris flies.
It is the best three seconds in the game.

■ Things to collect
· 7 ball skins — Classic, Gold, Shadow, Rainbow and more
· 5 trail effects — Stardust, Flame, Neon Ribbon
· 5 background themes — Aurora, Sunset, Deep Sea, Cyberpunk, Cherry Blossom
· 3 consumables — Revive ticket, Coin booster, Starting shield
All of it is earned with coins from playing. Nothing is paywalled.

■ A reason to come back
· 7-day check-in cycle with streak bonuses
· Daily missions that reshuffle every day, weekly missions that reset each week
· 6 lifetime achievements

■ Two-week season rankings
The all-time board is your hall of fame; the season board starts fresh every two weeks,
so it is never too late to climb. Clear the season target score to unlock a limited skin
that disappears when the season ends.

■ Plays offline
The game itself runs with no connection at all.
The network is only used for rankings and progress backup.

■ Switch phones without losing anything
Link a Google or Apple account and your best score, coins and skins come back
after a reinstall or on a new device.
Linking is entirely optional — every feature works without signing in.

■ How it is funded
Ad-supported, with no paid products of any kind.
Revives and double coins are rewarded ads you choose to watch.
Your first three runs are free of interstitials.

Find out how deep you can get.
```
